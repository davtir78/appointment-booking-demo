Book online

What to try

  1. Book, then change and cancel. Everything works with the keyboard alone.
  2. Lose a race. Open the widget’s “Demo controls”, tick “Another customer takes the time I’m about to hold”, then hold a time. The overlap rule rejects you and offers other times. (With the real API running, npm run demo, the controls are in the “Behind the scenes” panel under the widget, which also shows every request, the server’s events and the messages sent.)
  3. Run out of time. A held time lasts five minutes. Leave it, or choose “Keep holding” to extend it.
  4. Change the time zone. Pick Los Angeles or London under “Show times in”. The same bookings read differently; the clinic’s own zone stays the reference.

What this demo does not do

  • It makes no bookings and contacts no calendar, email or text service. It makes no network requests while you use it.
  • Nothing you type is sent, saved or kept. Reloading the page discards everything.
  • The clinic, the staff and their hours are invented.
